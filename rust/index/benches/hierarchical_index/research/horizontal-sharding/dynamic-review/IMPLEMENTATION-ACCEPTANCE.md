# POC acceptance and 100B scaling evidence

The goal is a working proof of concept and measurements that support a credible theoretical projection to 100 billion vectors. Production integration, save and recovery failures, node failures, fleet resizing and deployment hardening are outside acceptance. The historical receipts below remain evidence of their exact experiments; they do not add requirements to this scope.

The scaling POC may assume that its captured input objects remain fixed and immutable for the experiment. This is an explicit modeling assumption, not an enforced guarantee of the local filesystem or production storage provider. A result under that assumption must identify bootstrap/full-oracle work separately from changed-owner work. It must not claim that production storage implements version pins or retention leases.

## Measurements still needed for a credible 100B argument

1. **Changed-owner validation work.** Under explicitly fixed immutable inputs, measure bootstrap, affected-owner semantic audit, removed/replacement ID-union proof, unchanged-owner reuse and full oracle separately. Demonstrate exact ownership and output parity on the retained topology transition. This tests proportional work under the POC assumption; it does not require a production provider/GC implementation.
2. **Construction and append cost accounting.** Separate full canonical copying, all-owner cloning, record/namespace scans, affected-owner rewrite and durability work. A changed-owner validator does not make append proportional while those whole-corpus costs remain. Project storage, network and elapsed work using measured units and stated assumptions.
3. **Metadata and ownership scaling.** Account for owner descriptors, global level widths, frontier state, ownership paths, flat-map persistence and record-ID namespaces. The 100k prototype cap, 128-owner bounds and u32 local/record offsets must be addressed explicitly in the theoretical partitioning and metadata model; raising constants is not evidence.
4. **Operation workspace.** Report postings, exact candidate deduplication, all cutoff ties, rerank references, provider caches and output buffers separately. Extrapolate worst-case ties and requested-result storage as well as ordinary cases. Existing payload counters do not establish a total process-memory bound.
5. **Projection inputs and uncertainty.** Tie the 100B model to preserved construction/query measurements, representative dimensions and documented hardware assumptions. State sensitivity to owner size, block size, request size, skew and replication. No new query-strategy comparison or larger corpus run is authorized by this matrix.

## Excluded engineering and historical evidence

Provider-enforced immutability/retention, production SysDb/Log/RPC integration, deployment readiness, physical fleet execution, node failures, save-failure recovery, retries and fleet resizing are optional engineering concerns outside the requested POC acceptance. They must remain accurately labeled if discussed, but their absence does not block an explicitly assumed-immutable scaling experiment. The retained historical sections below describe earlier scopes and limits; current acceptance and priorities are defined above.
